# VRT_NOTES.md — Irish Vehicle Registration Tax reference

## What is VRT?

Vehicle Registration Tax is charged by Revenue Commissioners when registering 
a foreign car in Ireland. It is based on **OMSP** — Open Market Selling Price,
which is the price Revenue determines independently, NOT the price you paid.

**Key point:** For rare/classic JDM cars, Revenue may have no reference price 
and will assess OMSP based on condition inspection. Budget for surprises.

**Calculator:** https://www.revenue.ie/en/vrt/vrt-calculator.aspx

---

## VRT Rate Bands (modern cars, CO2-based)

| Band | CO2 (g/km) | VRT rate |
|------|-----------|---------|
| A0   | 0         | 7%      |
| A1   | 1–80      | 9%      |
| A2   | 81–100    | 14%     |
| A3   | 101–110   | 15.75%  |
| A4   | 111–120   | 17%     |
| B1   | 121–130   | 20%     |
| B2   | 131–140   | 21%     |
| C    | 141–155   | 23%     |
| D    | 156–170   | 25%     |
| E    | 171–190   | 30%     |
| F    | 191–225   | 35%     |
| G    | 226+      | 41%     |

**For pre-1980 classics:** Revenue usually applies a fixed reduced rate 
or category-specific formula. Call Revenue directly: 1800 99 1234.

**AE86 (4AGE, 1587cc):** Likely assessed in B1-B2 range (mid CO2) unless 
Revenue applies classic vehicle rules. Estimate 20–25% of OMSP.

## Customs Duty

- **Cars manufactured 30+ years ago**: may qualify as antiques under 
  CN 9705.00 — potentially 0% duty. Requires documentation proving age.
- **Standard rate (Ukraine non-EU origin)**: 6.5%
- **Post-Brexit / Ukraine Association Agreement**: verify current rules 
  with Revenue or a customs broker — rules changed post-2022.

---

## VAT

23% on (car price + customs duty). Applied at registration / import stage.

---

## NOx Levy

Applies only to cars first registered in Ireland FROM 1 January 2021.
Pre-2021 imports: **€0 NOx levy** — relevant for all AE86/classic imports.
